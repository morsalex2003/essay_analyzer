# EFLLex dataset

`EFLLex.tsv` is an unchanged copy downloaded from the official
[EFLLex resource page](https://cental.uclouvain.be/cefrlex/efllex/download/)
maintained by CENTAL at UCLouvain.

EFLLex describes the frequency distribution of English lemmas and
multiword expressions across pedagogical materials for learners at CEFR levels
A1, A2, B1, B2, and C1. Its level-frequency values are dispersion-adjusted,
normalized frequencies per million words; they are not probabilities and do
not assign one CEFR level to each word.

Please cite:

> Dürlich, L. and François, T. (2018). EFLLex: A Graded Lexical Resource for
> Learners of English as a Foreign Language. Proceedings of LREC 2018.
