I disagree with this point of view and I would like to add another reason no one mentioned. Farmland, housing and industry can develop easily in zones that already exist specifically for them. They can use sustainable methods for their development, but endangered species can’t. They need special and specific conditions to live, if we take from them this resources, unfortunately their life will end, which will slowly lead to the extinction of their species, which will also lead to changes in our planet. Anything or anyone has the right to decide for the future of life. We must keep balance in our planet. That’s why we should respect wildlife and the environment of our beloved planet earth, over expanding industries.
